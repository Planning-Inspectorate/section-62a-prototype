Application stages

This application is at the procedure decision stage.

Pre-application
Completed

The pre-application process for Section 62A applications is handled by the Planning Inspectorate.

The applicant sends the notice of intent to PINS with information on:

  • who the applicant is
  • the site address
  • the size of the development
  • expected submission date
  • Listed Building Consent status
  • and any likely issues with the development

These will be addressed in the application submission.

Accepted
Completed

The Planning Inspectorate (PINS) checks that the application is complete. For example, that the right documents and fees have been added.

Consultation
Completed

The Planning Inspectorate:

  • publishes the application documents on a dedicated web page
  • carries out any required consultations
  • may place a press notice

The relevant local planning authority displays a site notice and notifies any adjoining owner or occupier if needed.

Statutory consultees and other interested parties can send written representations to the Planning Inspectorate.

Written representations must be sent no less than 21 days from the date the application is accepted. Written representations submitted after this date may not be considered.

Procedure decision
Completed

The procedure is the method of decision process the Planning Inspectorate will use to make a decision about the proposed development.

The Planning Inspectorate will keep the procedure choice, including combined procedures, under review. Subject to any notification and procedural requirement, the procedure may be changed at any point before the decision is issued.

The planning inspector will determine how the application should proceed. The possible procedures are:

  • written representations
  • hearing

The decision will be communicated to the applicant, relevant Local Planning Authority (LPA) and anyone who submitted comments during the representations period.

Reporting and decision
Completed

The inspector uses the evidence to write a decision report about the outcome of the application.

The Planning Inspectorate (PINS) issues a formal decision notice including a statement with the reasons for the decision.

If the application is approved, the decision will also list any necessary conditions.

Post-decision
Completed

Where permission has been granted on a Section 62A application, the Local Planning Authority (LPA) is responsible for monitoring the implementation of the permission. The LPA must ensure that it is carried out in accordance with the plans and any planning conditions.

To challenge a decision on a Section 62A application, an application must be made to the High Court. This must be made within 6 weeks of the day the decision is issued.
