At the Planning Inspectorate, we value your privacy. This statement outlines how we process your personal data.

When you submit a written representation on a Section 62A application, we’ll ask for your name and email address.

Your name and written representation will be published on the Find a Section 62A application service. We will only use your email address to communicate with you about your submission and will not use your data for any other, unrelated purpose.

We’ll process all of your data in line with our legal obligation under The Town and Country Planning (Section 62A Applications) (Procedure and Written Representations) Order 2025.

You can read full details of how we process your data in our GOV.UK Customer Privacy Notice (opens in a new tab).

You have the right to access and correct your data. To do this, contact the Planning Inspectorate Senior Data Protection Manager at dataprotection@planninginspectorate.gov.uk.

S62A/2026/0048

Have your say

Use this service to:

  • send us your comments about this application
  • share any supporting attachments

This will make up your written representation.

We'll consider these before we make a decision on an application and we'll publish your name and comments on this website unless you have asked for your name to be withheld.

Before you start

We'll ask for your:

  • full name
  • email address

If you're commenting on behalf of someone else, we may ask for:

  • the name of the charity or organisation you work for
  • your job title or volunteer role
  • the name of the person or organisation you are representing
  • the names in your group representation

If you cannot complete your submission in one sitting, the session will last for 24 hours until you have to re-enter all your information.

Using artificial intelligence (AI) in written representations

If you use AI tools to help draft your written representation, check it carefully before you submit. Make sure the content accurately reflects your views and does not introduce any false or misleading information.
