If Planning Inspectors decide that written representations are not enough to reach a decision, a public hearing may be scheduled.

You do not commit to attending by choosing 'Yes', and you can change your mind later. We will send you details if a hearing is scheduled.

You do not have to attend for your comments to be considered.

What is a hearing?

A hearing is a round-table discussion lead by an inspector.

The hearing allows anyone to make oral representations. It also allows the appointed person to ask questions about points of fact or other questions they have.

A hearing is not for parties to test the evidence of others through cross examination or direct questioning.
