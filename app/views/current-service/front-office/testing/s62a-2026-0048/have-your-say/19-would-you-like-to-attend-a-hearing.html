You do not have to attend for your comments to be considered.

What is a hearing?

A hearing is a structured, face-to-face meeting. It's run as a round-table discussion led by an independent planning inspector.

At the start of the hearing, the inspector will:

  • explain the details of the application
  • state the main issues to be resolved
  • list the specific topics that need further explanation

The inspector will then lead a discussion on each topic. They will ask direct questions to both sides to better understand the case.

Most hearings last between half a day and a full day. They usually conclude with the inspector visiting the development site.
