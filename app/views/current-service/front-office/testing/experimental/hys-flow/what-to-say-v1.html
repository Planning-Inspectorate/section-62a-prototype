What do you want to say about this application?

Your comment can support, object or provide general feedback on the proposed development.

Once we have reviewed your comment we will publish it on the website with your name, and your organisation's name, if you have added it.

What to include in your comment

You should focus on planning-related matters. You can write relevant detail on how the development affects:

  • you, your business or the local community
  • the environment and local economy
  • transport links and access to services
  • quality of life, such as noise, lighting and living conditions

You must tell us in your comment if you’d like to attend a hearing if one takes place.

If you want to add any supporting documents to your comment, you can do this on the next page.

What not to include in your comment

! Warning Do not include in your comment any personal information that could identify you or another person.
Check what else you must not include

Do not include:

  • personal data such as names, addresses, contact details, signatures and photos that could identify you or anyone else
  • links to third party websites, except GOV.UK or chartered professional websites
  • sensitive information

Sensitive information refers to information that:

  • could identify a child
  • relates to health
  • relates to crime 

It also means any information relating to an individual's:

  • race or ethnic origin
  • political or religious beliefs
  • trade union membership
  • genetic or biometric data
  • physical characteristics
  • mental health or sexual health
  • sexual orientation and sex life

If you include any personal data, third party links or sensitive data in your comment or supporting documents, this will be redacted and will not be taken into account by the Planning Inspectors.

If you include any racist, inflammatory or abusive language, your written representation will be rejected.

Using AI in your comment

You can use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to help draft or translate your written representation. You must check that everything is accurate and reflects your own views.

You must tell us in your comment if you’d like to attend a hearing in the event that one is scheduled.
