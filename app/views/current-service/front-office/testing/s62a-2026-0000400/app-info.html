Application stages

This application is at the reporting and decision stage.

Pre-application
Completed

The pre-application process

Applicants must send a notice of intent to us before submitting a full application. This notice must include:

  • applicant contact details
  • the site address
  • the size of the development
  • the expected submission date
  • whether the proposal requires Listed Building Consent
  • any potential issues with the development
Validation
Completed

We check that the application is complete. We will check that applicants have:

  • provided the right documents
  • paid the application fee
Consultation
Completed

How consultation works

We will:

  • publish the application documents online
  • carry out consultations with required organisations
  • publish press notices if required

The relevant local planning authority will put up a site notice and notify neighbouring properties if required.

How to comment

Anyone can submit a comment on a planning application. You must submit your comments before the consultation deadline. We may not consider comments received after this date.

Procedure decision
Completed

How we examine planning applications

An Inspector or appointed person examines the application and makes the final decision.

They will decide whether to examine the application using one of two procedure types:

  • written representations (reviewing written comments and supporting attachments)
  • a hearing (a meeting led by the Inspector or appointed person)

We will notify you about the chosen procedure if you are:

  • the applicant
  • the local planning authority
  • anyone who submitted a comment

Changes to the process

We can change how an application is examined at any time before making a final decision.

Written representations
Completed

An Inspector or appointed person reviews the written representations (comments and feedback) and usually visits the site.

Reporting and decision
In progress

How the decision is made and published

The Inspector or appointed person uses the evidence collected to write a decision report.

We publish the decision notice online. The notice includes:

  • the reasons for the decision
  • any conditions attached to the permission, if approved
Post-decision
Not started

Monitoring approved developments

If the application is approved, the relevant local planning authority monitors the development.

They will check that the work follows the approved plans and any planning conditions.

Challenging a decision

You can apply to the High Court for a judicial review if you want to challenge the decision. You must submit your application to the High Court within 6 weeks of the decision date.
