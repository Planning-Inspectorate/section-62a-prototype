Application stages

This application is at the hearing stage.

Pre-application
Completed

The Planning inspectorate handles the pre-application process for s62A applications.

The applicant sends a notice of intent to the Planning Inspectorate with information on:

  • who the applicant is
  • the site address
  • the size of the development
  • the expected submission date
  • the Listed Building Consent status
  • any likely issues with the development

The applicant will address these details in their full application submission.

Accepted
Completed

The Planning Inspectorate checks that the application is complete. This includes making sure the right documents have been provided and any fees have been paid.

Consultation
Completed

The Planning Inspectorate:

  • publishes the application documents online
  • carries out consultations
  • places press notices when required

The relevant local planning authority displays a site notice and notifies any neighbours if needed.

Anyone can make a written representation to the Planning Inspectorate.

You must submit your comments within the representation period. We may not consider comments submitted after this date.

Procedure decision
Completed

The procedure is how the Planning Inspectorate will examine the application and make a final decision.

The appointed person will decide if the application should proceed using:

  • written representations
  • a hearing

The Planning Inspectorate can review and change this procedure at any time before they issue a decision.

They will tell the applicant, the local planning authority and anyone who commented which procedure they have chosen.

Hearing
In-progress

A hearing is a round-table discussion lead by an inspector.

The hearing allows anyone to make oral representations. It also allows the appointed person to ask questions about points of fact or other questions they have.

A hearing is not for parties to test the evidence of others through cross examination or direct questioning.

Reporting and decision
Not started

The appointed person uses the evidence to write a decision report.

The Planning Inspectorate will then publish a formal decision notice on this website. This notice includes:

  • the reasons for the decision
  • any required conditions (if the application is approved)
Post-decision
Not started

If the application is approved, the local planning authority is responsible for monitoring the development. They must make sure the work follows the approved plans and any planning conditions.

To challenge a decision, you must apply to the High Court. You must do this within 6 weeks of the decision date.
