Cookies are files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website. We use strictly necessary cookies to enable essential parts of this website, and optional cookies to store information about how you use the Find a local planning application made to the Planning Inspectorate service, such as the pages you visit.

Cookie settings

We use different types of cookies.

You can choose which cookies you're happy for us to use and how the data is shared with the Planning Inspectorate.

Strictly necessary cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies, also known as essential cookies, to help you use the Find a local planning application made to the Planning Inspectorate service. Strictly necessary cookies do not collect any form of personally identifiable data or track your browsing habits.

Strictly necessary cookies do things such as:

  • remember your progress through the service
  • remember you have seen the cookies message

Cookies used to improve the Find a local planning application made to the Planning Inspectorate service

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how you use the Find a local planning application made to the Planning Inspectorate service so we can improve it based on user needs. These cookies provide information such as the pages you visit, how many people use our website, and what country you access our website from. We do not allow Google to use or share the data about how you use this site. View our Customer Privacy Notice for more information.
